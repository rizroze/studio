# RIZZY TODAY

> This is it. This is me.

A living note on what this site *is now*, the mindset behind it, and where it goes next. Not a spec — a stance.

---

## The shift

For a long time the work needed a title. So I let people call it freelancing, design, content, "do you do logos?" — and the site sold that. Bookshelf, pricing, "book a call." A shop.

I'm done running a shop.

I started in guerrilla marketing for underground rock bands. Self-taught tattooing. Studied engineering and film, then unlearned most of it. Ten years following the work wherever it went — runway, illustration, motion, brand systems, content direction — and the last four leading creative in Solana. The work was here before any title. It's the intersection of everything I've been doing my whole life. I was just following my heart, and realized they call that "Creative Director" these days.

So the site stopped selling and started *being*.

---

## The stance

- **Capability, not client.** The work is organized by what it *is* — **Direction · Design · Product** — not by who paid for it. Anyone can read a logo wall. Few can see a whole creative operation.
- **Quiet conviction.** No funnel, no "let's work together!", no urgency tricks. Full white, calm, editorial. If you want to be in a room with me, the work already told you.
- **Show the range, and the play.** The serious work lives in the disciplines. The Lab shows I build things for the love of it — an iPod on a cable, liquid glass you can drag, a tamagotchi that lives on the page. That's not a gimmick. That's the proof.
- **Findable, not flexed.** Testimonials exist (References), tucked away for the curious founder doing diligence — never shoved in your face.

The whole site is one sentence: *here's the work, here's me, I'm available — your move.*

**Polish**
- Keep the share card sharp; revisit OG art when the brand mark evolves.
- Watch the heavy media weight (videos) — recompress the biggest if first-load ever feels heavy on mobile.

**The real plan**
- Keep making things. Keep them here. Let the work do the talking.
- The site's job isn't to convince anyone. It's to be true. The rest takes care of itself.
